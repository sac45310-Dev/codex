- Individuals are never contacted; organisations are approached.
- Agents never record or infer any individual's demographic or identity
  attributes. The keyword rule is org-level only.
- `faith_orientation` and `do_not_pursue` are owner-set; agents flag, never classify.
- Initials-only, first-name-only and codename records are never emitted and
  never resolved from another source.
- A hold by any agent binds every agent; never resolve by majority.
- Per-agent yield is not wave yield; recompute against the CRM after merge.
- A tool's prose summary is not a tool result; read the URL list.
- Negatives are amended (`reversed`), never deleted.
