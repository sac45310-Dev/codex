## Rules applied

1. **Organization-shaped name → reclassify, don't reject.** 222 rows filed as
   `individual` were organizations ("Awaken Church Las Vegas", "LifeWay",
   "The Gospel Coalition", "Duke Divinity School"). Moved to `organization`.
2. **Salaried ≠ support-raised.** Seminary faculty, presidents, published
   authors, commercial recording artists and parachurch executives were
   rejected on ICP grounds. They draw a salary or royalties; they do not manage
   a personal donor base. This covered the 23 `Dr.`-prefixed rows, 23 more from
   the identical source query, 24 musicians and 34 parachurch staff.
3. **No surname → reject, and do not resolve it.** 15 first-name-only rows.
   Several sat under an "international ministry leaders" query and may be
   deliberately anonymized sensitive-region workers, where inferring a surname
   would undo a protection the sending agency put there on purpose.
4. **A shared org URL is not evidence about a person.** 21 Acts 29 rows all
   carried the identical `https://acts29.com`; 2 Gospel Coalition rows the same.
5. **Deceased people go in `hunt_negatives`, not the queue.** Derek Kidner
   (1913–2008) and Elisabeth Elliot (1926–2015), both verified.

## Where the proposed plan was wrong

**Dedupe by name would have destroyed live prospects.** The plan was "keep the
higher-scored copy, reject the twin". In fact **11 of 37 same-name groups are
different people at different agencies**: David Price at WWNTBM *and* FOCUS,
Emily Williams at FOCUS *and* RUF, Mike Brown at Baptist Mid-Missions *and*
WGM, Jason Thomas at InterVarsity *and* BMFP, Noah Lee at WGM *and* Campus
Outreach, Scott Arnold at YFC *and* Reach Beyond.

The rule that actually works: **dedupe only within one registrable domain**
(or where one row has no citation at all), and keep the copy whose URL contains
the person's surname. Preferring the higher `fit_score` is wrong — it keeps
`/about-us` over `/missionaries/detail/overton`. 26 rows deduped, 11 groups left
intact.

**A category sweep nearly deleted real ICP.** Six rows inside the final
"clear it" group were genuine Ethnos360 missionaries on real per-person pages
(`blogs.ethnos360.org/<first>-<surname>/`) at the most-worked agency in the
database. Inspect a group before clearing it, even when the surrounding rows
are junk.

## The find: Wycliffe was written off in error

`hunt_negatives` id 322 recorded Wycliffe as `platform_not_person` in
w2026-09-10n. **That was wrong.** Nine legacy rows carry live per-person giving
pages — `/partner/bryan-lori-jones`, `/partner/esthermorrow`,
`/partner/garrettandleahharrison`, `/partner/rice`, `/partner/dstroyer`,
`/partner/fredjuanitaduong`, `/partner/DonWendyTin` — alongside opaque
Missionary IDs (`/partner/2439a4`, `/partner/1E84F6`). Wycliffe is a
**mixed-slug agency** like Converge and BBFI; the earlier probe saw only the
opaque half and generalised from it.

`wycliffe.org/partner/missionaries` **paginates to at least `?page=111`**, which
makes it plausibly the largest untouched seam in the project — larger than BBFI.
Target id 3 reopened at priority 88. The negative was amended rather than
deleted so the error stays visible.

Two caveats carried into the target notes: Wycliffe states some missionaries
withhold their details for security, so withheld, initials-only and ID-only
records go to `needs_review` and are never resolved from elsewhere; and couples
share one page and are not split in the legacy rows.
