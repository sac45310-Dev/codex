Both were recommended at the end of the waves t–z session and authorised afterwards.
Neither spends agent budget: they are database queries plus two web searches.

Reversal tables: `sales.audit_20260912_reversal` (scout_candidates),

## Audit 1 — holdings check keyed on org name

### Why

Waves y and z were redundant because I checked whether a per-person *surface*
existed and not whether we already *held* the people. Wave z ran the holdings
check and still failed, because I keyed it on `aim.org` when AIM's giving domain
is `aimint.org`. A hand-typed identifier that returns nothing is not evidence of
absence.

This audit does the check the way it should have been done: for every one of the
824 rows in `hunt_targets`, link person records by three independent paths and
take the union.

| path | how | reliability |
|---|---|---|
| **FK** | `scout_candidates.hunt_target_id` | exact, but only 704 of 8,703 rows carry it |
| **name** | normalised `meta->>'target_org'` vs normalised `org_name` | reliable; 5,830 rows carry `target_org` |
| **domain** | registrable domain of `source_url` vs of `website` | *unreliable* — see below |

### Result

6,691 person records, 5,900 of them linked to 173 distinct targets.

| roster_status | targets | hold people (FK or name) | hold people (domain only) | hold nothing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| unrostered | 572 | **32** | 17 | 523 |
| rejected | 130 | 5 | 1 | 124 |
| rostered | 73 | 72 | 1 | 0 |
| exhausted | 27 | 22 | 1 | **4** |
| partial | 22 | 21 | 1 | 0 |

**32 targets were marked `unrostered` with `headcount_found = 0` while already
holding person records.** The largest are not small:

| id | agency | held | approved | had read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 817 | Baptist International Missions (BIMI) | 153 | 153 | unrostered, 0 |

| 843 | Africa Inland Mission | 82 | 76 | unrostered, 0 |

AIM appears here with `website = usgiving.aimint.org`. **The correct domain was

These 32 are now `roster_status = 'partial'` with `headcount_found` backfilled and
a note naming the reversal table. `unrostered` fell 572 → 540, `partial` rose 22 → 54.

### What domain matching gets wrong

Domain matching alone produced 17 extra "holdings" and most are artefacts:

- **93 targets share `usachurches.org`** as their website — a church directory, not
  their own site. Any domain-keyed check collapses all 93 together.
- Five Campus Outreach locals share `cocolumbia.org/staff`; six share
  `comemphis.org/staff`. The same 21 (and 9) people match all of them.
- `Ratio Christi - Board` (233) picks up 59 people who belong to `Ratio Christi` —
  two targets, one organisation. **Dedupe candidate, not a status change.**

Domain-only matches were deliberately left alone. The rule this yields:
**key the holdings check on org name; use the domain only to confirm, never to decide.**

## Audit 2 — short-term participants among already-approved people

### Why

Encompass World Partners serves short-term trip participants and career
missionaries from one `/person/` path, and four such records were already
approved. Charity Reist, approved at 9, is a two-month trip participant.

### Method and what it found

Text signal across `summary`, `fit_reason`, `meta->>'role'` and review notes for
short-term vocabulary, plus a URL-path scan for trip-shaped namespaces.
137 of 6,691 person records carried any signal. Almost all are false positives,
and the false positives are the interesting part.

**The 78 RUF "intern" hits are not short-term.** The RUF Intern programme is a
two-year, full-time, personally support-raised role — one record even reads
"completed a two-year RUF internship". They are squarely in ICP. *Anyone running
a keyword sweep for "intern" would have destroyed 78 good prospects.*

**The `/team` URL hits are staff directories, not trip teams** — `converge.org/who-we-are/team/`,
`cosandiego.org/team`, `familylife.com/about/team/`. The URL-path scan found nothing.

**The BIMI "trip" hits are career missionaries who *host* trips.** Sean Lunday leads
BIMI Connect trips; Mark Gerosin has been church planting in Medellín since 2009.
Hosting a trip is evidence of a durable field role, not against it.

Only **two approved records survived as genuine short-term participants**, and both
were already scored 5 — the lowest band in the file:

| person | agency | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Gary Scharping | Training Leaders International | own record reads "Short-Term Team Member"; cited to `trainingleadersinternational.org/give?tbl=teams`, a team listing, not a personal page. TLI's own site confirms it sends **recurring short-term teams**. |
| Rebecca Asi | Ethnos360 | own record reads "Short-term missionary intern" |

Both returned to `pending`. Two more — **Andy Lamb** (CMDA, volunteer trip leader,
50+ trips, private-practice physician) and **Dr. Ashish Patel** (Flying Doctors,
board adviser) — are a *different* ICP miss: not short-term, but **no personal
giving page at all**. Their own `fit_reason` already says so. Left approved and
annotated; that boundary is the owner's call, as with the three Encompass records
flagged in wave y.

**The short-term problem is smaller than expected and the existing fit_score already
caught it.** Every genuine hit scored 5.

### What the audit found instead: a competitor already installed

Chasing the hypothesis that a shared namespace means mixed short-term traffic, the
largest generic namespace after Encompass turned out to be **`modernday.org/profile/`
— 109 person records, 96 of them approved.**

The hypothesis was wrong. Modern Day Missions is not a trip platform. It is a
missionary-support organisation of roughly 1,700 missionaries in 100 nations, and
its own site describes *"a proprietary donor management platform"* with
*"state of the art giving software"* where *"donors can manage their donations and
review their donations history."*

**That is a direct competitor to DonorSend, and 96 approved prospects are already on it.**

This is not a disqualification — a missionary on a platform they did not choose and
may not like is a legitimate prospect, and knowing the incumbent is an advantage in
the conversation. But it is a fact the pipeline was not capturing at all:
`hunt_targets.crm_incumbent` exists and is **empty on all 67 rows that have it set**,
and Modern Day appears in neither `hunt_targets` nor `hunt_negatives`.

All 109 records now carry `meta.platform_incumbent = 'Modern Day Missions'`. Status unchanged.

### Citation quality

Nine person records are cited to **organisation directory profiles** — eight to
`guidestar.org/profile/`, one to `bbb.org`. A GuideStar profile is a record of an
organisation and cannot evidence an individual's support-raised role. Annotated
with `meta.citation_quality = 'org_directory'`; status unchanged, re-cite before outreach.

## Rules this produces

1. **Key the holdings check on org name and the FK. Use the domain only to confirm.**
   Read the domain out of `hunt_targets.website`; never type it from memory.
2. **An empty result from a hand-typed identifier is not evidence of absence.**
3. **Never sweep on a keyword alone.** "Intern" would have cost 78 good RUF prospects;
   "trip" would have cost the BIMI missionaries who host them.
4. **Record the incumbent platform.** A per-person giving page is evidence of ICP fit
   *and* evidence of what the prospect already runs on.
5. **A person cited to an organisation directory is not evidenced.**
