# Wave w2026-09-11w — Mission to the World (MTW)

**4 agents, 72 searches. 380 unique people found, 376 loaded.**
**Yield 5.2 new people per query — the best rate in the project to date**, ahead
of Wycliffe's 4.2 and roughly four times the retirement line.

A fresh target: zero people held before this wave.

## Found by probing, not by planning

MTW was not on the wave list. It surfaced while probing candidates after the
Wycliffe continuation collapsed, and it beat every agency that *was* on the list.
The PCA's sending agency states **636 missionaries serving one year or longer
across 100 countries**, plus 1,101 short-term.

## Pattern

`mtw.org/missionaries/details/<slug>`, with **two slug shapes on one site**:

- `<surname>-<first>-<first>` — `meadows-amanda`, `crews-virginia`, `mccall-john-lorena`
- `<first>-and-<first>-<surname>` — `joe-and-felicity-congdon`, `darren-and-allison-terpstra`

Plus a few numeric IDs (`/details/14701`, `/details/400513`, graded `medium`) and
prefixed slugs for national partners and international associates
(`poland-national-partner-tomasz-otremba`, `mx-ptr-jorge-aleman`).

**My brief was wrong on this point** — it told the agents MTW used a surname-first
slug, singular. Harmless only because the same brief forbade slug guessing.

The `itinerating-missionaries` path was the most on-profile idea of the wave:
it lists people *currently raising support*, which is precisely the ICP.

## Protection handling was the best of any wave so far

MTW states plainly that some missionaries serve in **secure locations** and asks
donors to email rather than browse for them. All four agents handled this
without prompting: **15 initials-only or numeric-coded entries were routed to
`needs_review`, and not one reached a people array.** Charles & Irene T.,
Jim & Karen W., Keith & Debbie K., DH Henry, plus coded-slug cases.

I initially accused the roles agent of failing this exact rule. That accusation
was wrong — I had audited the file mid-write, before its completion notification
arrived, and drew a conclusion from a partial snapshot. The finished file routes
all six correctly. **Do not audit an agent's output before it reports done.**

## Verification

380 records from 72 searches is a suspiciously good return, so citations were
spot-checked rather than trusted: **6 of 6 sampled URLs confirmed live with
matching titles** (`joe-and-felicity-congdon`, `hugh-and-martine-wessel`,
`darren-and-allison-terpstra`, `matt-and-caroline-newkirk`, `mccall-john-lorena`,
`jacobson-katie`). Cross-slice overlap was 31% — 622 raw to 380 unique.

## Withheld

Four, on the closed-date-range rule refined in wave t:

- **Quentin Nantz** — "Christ's College president 2008-2017", no current role
- **Pamyla Burrack** — "Madrid 1993-2014 (historical)", agent itself added "may still be active"
- **Jay and Nancy Matsinger** — "now retired as associate missionaries but still active"

The Matsingers are the interesting case: still serving, but *retired associate*
status means they are not building a personal donor base. Still-active service
does not make a retiree a prospect — the same reasoning applied to Baptist
Church Planters retirees in wave q.

Kept deliberately: **Clay Quarterman** (Portugal 1977-1991 is historical, he
currently serves Ukraine) and **Hugh Wessel** (with MTW since 1979, former
country director, but currently serving France and Germany). 20 loaded records
carry `tenure_check_suggested`.

## Not exhausted

183 distinct person-pages against a stated 636 long-term roster. Unlike Wycliffe,
the shortfall here is not obviously an indexing wall — country and role
qualifiers were still producing when budget ran out. A second MTW wave is
defensible, but on wave u's evidence it should be treated as a hypothesis to
test cheaply, not a certainty.
